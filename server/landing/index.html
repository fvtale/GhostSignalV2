DataRail · Ghost 93.3 · Bryant Park, NYC

The city is holding
a song hostage.

GhostSignal is a location-based audio game played through smart glasses, or right here in your browser. Eight fragments of one track are buried around Bryant Park. Walk the route, find each signal by sound alone, and put the broadcast back together — before HUSH, the system erasing the city's sound, notices you.

Play the game Download for Android Your account

Free to play. Headphones recommended; the glasses are the full experience.

The run

Played on foot, by ear.

  1. 01

    Hunt by sound

    Each fragment plays from where it is buried. It clears as you close in — turn your head and it stays put in the world, like a real source.

  2. 02

    Answer out loud

    Passphrases, choices, rhythms tapped on the touchpad, questions asked of the characters riding your radio. Your voice is the controller.

  3. 03

    Build the track

    Every captured signal joins the mix as a new layer, on the next bar. Finish the run and the song plays whole — then the secret soundtrack unlocks.
